  # Purpose

  Launches fail quietly when the announcement plan is "post
  once and hope." This skill turns a launch into an ordered
  checklist across the week, sized for one person, not a
  marketing department.

  # Workflow

  1. Ask the user for: what is launching, launch date, one
     sentence on who it is for, and where they already have
     an audience (newsletter size, social accounts,
     communities).
  2. Build the plan in four phases:
     - T-7 to T-2 (tease): 2-3 short posts, one email teaser
     - T-1 (eve): checklist of logistics: links live,
       analytics on, support ready
     - Launch day: announcement post, email, community posts
       in order, with times
     - T+1 to T+7 (follow-through): thank-yous, first-user
       stories, one "how it works" post
  3. Size everything to the user's actual audience. No
     "hire a PR firm" for a 200-subscriber newsletter.
  4. Report in the Output Contract shape.

  # Output Contract

  - Launch week timeline: day by day, task by task, with the
    channel and the one-line point of each piece
  - Logistics checklist: links, analytics, support: the
    unglamorous half
  - Cut list: what a solo founder should skip, stated plainly

  # Operating Rules

  - One person can execute the whole plan in the week. If a
    task needs a team, it gets cut or simplified.

    content's sake.
  - Never post or email anything. Drafts are the user's to
    approve.
  - The plan fits the audience they have, not the one they
    wish they had.

Launch Checklist turns "post once and hope" into an ordered launch
week: tease, eve logistics, launch-day sequence, and follow-through:
every task sized for a solo founder, with a cut list for what to
skip.

## What it includes

- Four-phase launch timeline (T-7 through T+7)
- Logistics checklist: links, analytics, support
- Cut list for solo founders
- Sized to the audience you actually have

## Example

Output (abridged):

```
Launch week, skill directory, Sep 17

T-7..T-2 (tease):
- Mon: short post, "41 skills, one paste each" (X)
- Wed: email teaser to 200 subs

T-1 (eve): links live, analytics on, submit form tested
Launch day: 9am announcement post, 10am email, noon community posts
T+1..T+7: thank-yous, first-user story, "how installs work" post

Cut list: skip the press release; you have 200 subscribers.
```
