House projects run for months, and the quotes, costs, and next steps scatter across email threads, contractor texts, and a folder of receipts. This Space keeps every project on one board in your sidebar: what is planned, what was quoted, what it cost so far, and what is waiting on whom.

Each project gets a card with its name, room or area, status, and target date. Inside the card you compare quotes side by side: contractor, price, and what the price covers. The cost tally totals what you entered against the project budget, and the next-step list names the one thing holding each project up, whether that is a permit, a part, or a call back from a contractor.

It pairs well with the contractor-quote-requester skill: draft the quote request in chat, then compare the replies here once they come in.

## What it includes

- Project cards with name, room, status, and target date
- Quote comparison per project with contractor, price, and scope notes
- Cost tally showing spent against budget per project and overall
- Next-step list naming what each project is waiting on

## Example

You track three projects at once: a shed, a fireplace insert, and new patio cushions. The board shows the shed quoted at $2,900 with assembly still to price, the insert waiting on a depth re-measure, and the cushions waiting on one last measurement, with nothing spent so far and a $5,000 combined budget.
