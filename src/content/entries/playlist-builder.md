Playlist Builder is a prompt pack for the event that needs music
with a shape. Give it the event, vibe, and length; get a playlist
brief in four arcs, opener, build, peak, landing, with every song
placed for flow and three alternates in case the mood shifts.

## What it includes

- Four-arc sequencing with per-song placement notes
- Flow rules: no jarring genre jumps
- Crowd-fit filtering
- Three swap-in alternates

## Example

Input: "Backyard BBQ, easygoing, 3 hours, mixed ages."

Output (abridged):

```
Opener: "Brown Eyed Girl", familiar, sets the easy tone.
Build: "Levitating" into "Uptown Funk", energy climbs.
Peak: "Dancing Queen", the 20 minutes everyone remembers.
Landing: "Harvest Moon", wind down as the fire dies.

Alternates: "Good Vibrations", "Island in the Sun", "September".
```
