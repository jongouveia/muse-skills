Photo Caption Writer is a prompt pack for the photo that's ready
but the words aren't. Five options per photo, straightforward,
funny, minimal, question-driven, and storytelling, matched to your
voice and the platform, with hashtags listed separately and never
crammed into the caption.

## What it includes

- Five style-varied caption options
- Voice matching to your past posts
- Hashtags listed separately
- No invented photo details

## Example

Input: a golden-hour deck photo for Instagram.

Output (abridged):

```
1. Straightforward: "September evenings on the deck."
2. Funny: "The deck has entered its golden era."
3. Minimal: "Golden hour, earned."
4. Question: "Where are you watching the sun go down tonight?"
5. Story: "Three summers of projects and this is the payoff..."

Hashtags: #goldenhour #decklife #newengland ...
```
