Cold Outreach Drafter is a prompt pack for prospecting that doesn't
read like a template. Feed it real research on the lead; get a
sub-120-word email with a specific opener that proves you did the
work, one problem sentence, one proof point, and one low-friction
ask. Thin research gets flagged, not faked.

## What it includes

- Research-proven opener requirement
- Sub-120-word structure with proof point
- Banned-phrase rules (no "hope this finds you well")
- Thin-research flag instead of faked personalization

## Example

Input: research notes on a record store owner with a slow site.

Output (abridged):

```
Subject: your site on mobile

Saw the shop's Instagram is driving traffic but the site takes
9 seconds to load on mobile, that's where the drop-off lives.

I rebuild slow retail sites; last one went from 8s to 1.4s and
online orders doubled in a quarter.

Worth a 15-minute look at what's slowing yours down?
```
