A record collection grows past memory faster than any shelf does, and the cost of forgetting what you own is buying the same album twice at a fair. This Space keeps the collection and the want list together in your sidebar, searchable in seconds while you stand in a shop.

Each record you own carries its artist, title, format, condition, price paid, and the date you added it, grouped by artist so a browse feels like flipping through the crates. The want list holds the records still hunted, with a target price beside each one, so a deal is easy to judge on the spot. The search box covers both lists at once, which is what stops the duplicate buy.

It pairs well with the deal-hunting habit of checking prices before you buy: look the record up, then check here whether it is already yours.

## What it includes

- Collection list grouped by artist, with format, condition, price paid, and date added per record
- Want list with artist, title, and target price per record
- Search box across both lists

## Example

After logging a bulk buy of 40 records, the collection shows 212 records across 130 artists. In a shop you search "Rumours" and see it is already owned in VG+ condition, bought for $8, so you pass and check the want list instead.
