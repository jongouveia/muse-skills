  ## The prompt

  Write the SEO metadata for this page. I will give you a URL or a
  topic, plus the primary keyword.

  1. If I give a URL, read the page first and base the copy on
     what it actually says. If I give a topic, ask one clarifying
     question only if the angle is unclear.
  2. Title tag: under 60 characters, keyword near the front,
     written as a promise of what the page delivers.
  3. Meta description: 150-160 characters, keyword included
     naturally, ending with the reason to click.
  4. Give two options for each, labeled A and B, with different
     angles (one direct, one curiosity-driven).
  5. Show the character count next to each.

  Rules: no keyword stuffing, no clickbait that the page can't
  deliver on. If the page is thin, say what it would need before
  the metadata matters.

SEO Meta Writer writes title tags and meta descriptions that fit the
character limits and sound human. Give it a URL or a topic plus your
keyword; get two options for each, with counts shown, in a direct
and a curiosity-driven flavor.

## What it includes

- Title tags under 60 characters, keyword-forward
- Meta descriptions at 150-160 characters with a reason to click
- Two angles per element (direct / curiosity)
- Honest "the page is too thin for this to matter" flag

## Example

Input: "topic: sourdough starter guide, keyword: sourdough starter."

Output (abridged):

```
Title A (54): Sourdough Starter Guide: From Flour to First Loaf
Title B (47): Your Sourdough Starter, Minus the Guesswork
Meta A (156): A sourdough starter in 7 days with nothing but flour,
  water, and patience. Feeding schedule, troubleshooting, and your
  first loaf — start tonight.
```
