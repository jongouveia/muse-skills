Social Repurposer takes one piece of writing and turns it into three
platform-native versions: a numbered X thread with a real hook, a
conversational LinkedIn post that ends in a question, and a
short-form caption with hashtags. Same idea, three shapes, your
voice intact.

## What it includes

- X thread (5-8 posts, hook first, takeaway last)
- LinkedIn post (150 words, readable line breaks)
- Short-form caption with up to 5 hashtags
- Voice matching to the source

## Example

Input: a 600-word newsletter section on inbox zero being a trap.

Output (abridged):

```
Core idea: Inbox zero optimizes the wrong thing : responsiveness
over importance.

X thread:
1/ Inbox zero is a trap. It optimizes for responsiveness, not
importance. Here's what to do instead...
[...]

LinkedIn: I spent a year at inbox zero. My reward? Faster replies
to emails that didn't matter. [...]

Caption: Inbox zero optimizes the wrong metric. Try the 2-folder
system instead. #productivity #inboxzero
```
