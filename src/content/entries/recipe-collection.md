Recipes worth keeping end up scattered across notes, screenshots, cookbooks, and printouts, and the one you want is never the one you can find. This Space gathers them into a single browsable board in your sidebar, each with its ingredients, steps, times, and servings, so dinner planning starts with a search instead of a hunt.

Each recipe card carries its title, source, prep and cook time, servings, and tags for cuisine and course, so you can filter to weeknight chicken or a slow Sunday roast in one tap. The search box covers titles, ingredients, and tags at once, which is handy when the question is what to cook with the chicken thighs already in the fridge. A tried flag and a short note record what worked and what to change next time, so a good recipe gets better each time you cook it.

It pairs well with the recipe-cooking-companion skill: the skill writes a dish you cook from memory as a repeatable recipe, and this Space is where that recipe lives afterward.

## What it includes

- Recipe cards with title, source, prep time, cook time, servings, tags, ingredients, and steps
- Search box across titles, ingredients, and tags
- Tried flag and short note per recipe for what worked and what to change

## Example

You save 24 recipes over a month, 9 of them tagged weeknight. On a Tuesday you search "chicken thighs" and get 4 matches; you pick the one marked tried with a note that says add extra lemon next time, and dinner is planned in under a minute.
